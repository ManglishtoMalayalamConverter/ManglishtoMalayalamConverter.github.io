About the Manglish to Malayalam Converter

Manglish is the practice of using English letters to write Malayalam words phonetically. This style has become incredibly popular for texting and social media, allowing users to type in Malayalam without a dedicated keyboard. Our free tool is designed to bridge this gap effortlessly, instantly and accurately converting Manglish text into the native Malayalam script.

This tool operates entirely on your device (client-side), which means the text you enter never leaves your browser. This ensures lightning-fast performance and complete privacy for your data.

How to Use the Tool

  1. Enter Text: Type or paste your Manglish text into the left input box.
  2. Press Translate: Click the "Translate" button located below the boxes.
  3. Get the Result: The converted Malayalam text will instantly appear in the right box.
  4. Copy the Text: Use the copy icon in the top-right corner of the output box to copy the text to your clipboard.
